Atul Butte was my PhD advisor. He died in 2025, fighting a complex cancer. He is deeply missed by all of us who knew him.

He was a scientist, a visionary, a leader, and an entrepreneur. And by the end, most importantly, a great person.

It is a deep honor to have been his student. My life will be devoted to AI for medicine.

He was the Priscilla Chan and Mark Zuckerberg Distinguished Professor at UCSF and the inaugural director of the [Bakar Computational Health Sciences Institute](https://bakarinstitute.ucsf.edu/). A pediatrician and biomedical informatician, he spent his career making data useful for patients, and he taught a generation of trainees to think that way.

I completed my PhD in Computational Biology at UCSF under his co-advising. These photographs are from that day.

UCSF published a [remembrance of his life and work](https://www.ucsf.edu/news/2025/06/430236/bioinformatics-pioneer-atul-butte-dies-at-55).
